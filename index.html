FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Still unsure? Message us on Instagram and we'll reply the same day.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. We build a working demo of your homepage at no cost and with no obligation. If you'd like the full site, we continue. If not, no hard feelings.

How long does a website take?

Starter sites take about 5 days and Growth sites about 7 days after we receive your content. Pro projects usually take 10 to 14 days.

Do I need to provide text and photos?

It helps, but it isn't required. We can write your copy (included in Pro) and source professional, licensed stock photos until you have your own.

What about hosting and my domain?

We help you buy a domain (around $10 to $15 a year) and host your site on fast, reliable infrastructure. The Care plan covers hosting, updates and edits.

How does the AI chat assistant work?

It's trained on your services, prices and FAQs, so it answers customers instantly and points them to booking. You can review and update its answers at any time.

How do payments work for international clients?

We invoice in USD, GBP or INR and accept international transfers via Wise or PayPal. You pay 50% to start and 50% when you're happy and ready to launch.
