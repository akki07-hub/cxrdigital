# CXR Digital — Agency Website

**Build Smart. Grow Digital.** The official website for CXR Digital, a studio building modern business websites with online booking, AI chat assistants and lead capture.

## Sections
- Hero with live demo previews, plus a "Get a free demo" call to action
- **Live demos**: 5 working client-style sites, filterable by industry
- Services, **pricing with a USD / GBP / INR switcher** (picked automatically from the visitor's time zone), the 7-day process, why us, FAQ
- **Free demo request form**, which sends to Formspree when connected and falls back to Instagram DM

## Connect the contact form
1. Create a free form at [formspree.io](https://formspree.io) using your business email.
2. Paste the form URL into `FORM_ENDPOINT` at the top of `app.js`.

## Update the demo screenshots
The screenshots in `img/` were captured from the live demos. Replace any `.webp` file (1200×750) to update it.
